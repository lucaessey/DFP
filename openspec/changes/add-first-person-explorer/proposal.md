# Proposal

## Why

Players want to explore DFP at character eye level without changing the management game, their saves, or their upgrades. A permanent optional outfit makes this perspective easy to purchase and switch off.

## What Changes

- Add the $1,000 First-Person Explorer outfit with permanent ownership and free equipment changes.
- Provide eye-level cameras, camera-relative movement, mouse look and simultaneous touch movement/look across eleven business floors, the basement lounge, and Story Mode scenes.
- Show low-corner hands and compact carried goods with exact quantities, retaining the existing inventory and automatic work rules.
- Preserve the existing computer and security views and restore the gameplay position and heading on return.
- Migrate saves safely, bundle procedural assets for offline play, and verify desktop/mobile views.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `first-person-explorer`: Optional purchasable perspective outfit, controls, presentation and persistence across existing game scenes.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The repository has no main specs; existing outfit, economy, story and pet requirements remain in their prior change artifacts.

## Impact

Outfit configuration, save migration, movement inputs, business/story/lounge renderers, input handling and presentation. No new dependencies or services; no economy changes, deployment or publishing.
